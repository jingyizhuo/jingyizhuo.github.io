I am currently a postdoctoral research scientist at Princeton University's High Meadows Environmental Institute, working with Prof. [Gabriel Vecchi](https://vecchi.princeton.edu/people/gabriel-vecchi). Before this, I was at Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory of Columbia University, working with [Chia-Ying Lee](https://lamont.columbia.edu/directory/chia-ying-lee), [Adam H. Sobel](https://www.apam.columbia.edu/faculty/adam-sobel), [Suzana J. Camargo](https://www.ldeo.columbia.edu/~suzana/index.html), and [Richard Seager](https://ocp.ldeo.columbia.edu/res/div/ocp/people/seager/) (a true dream team). I received both my B.S. and Ph.D. in atmospheric science from Nanjing University under the supervision of Prof. [Zhe-Min Tan](https://as.nju.edu.cn/as_en/0a/7c/c20737a330364/page.htm).

Research Area(s):  Climate science; extreme weather events; tropical cyclones; ocean-atmosphere interaction; detection and attribution; physics-informed machine learning
